# Veilbound 0.1.60 client playtest checklist

This checklist covers the visual and interaction behavior that headless CI cannot prove. Use the exact `Veilbound-0.1.60-dev.jar` produced by the validation workflow.

## Boundary and fog

- Confirm every Domain boundary face is completely opaque black with no purple/blue speckling.
- Walk and strafe while watching the fog: the procedural field must remain world-locked rather than moving with the camera/player.
- Confirm fog rolls inward through roughly three blocks, with the strongest bank nearest the wall and visible dissipation farther inward.
- Confirm noise breaks up obvious slice/pixel banding without creating shimmering tied to player motion.
- Approach every boundary orientation, including floor and ceiling: fog should clear within about one block of the camera instead of rendering in the player's face.
- Check frame time beside large boundaries and compare against an ordinary Domain interior; watch specifically for translucent-overdraw spikes.
- Confirm adjoining boundary faces do not expose bright seams or gaps at edges/corners.

## Ores and animated blocks

- Verify all Veilbound ores have both stone and deepslate variants where applicable.
- Confirm animated ore textures advance smoothly and do not bleed between frames.
- Inspect animated engineered blocks at close and medium distance for atlas/frame artifacts.

## Engineering monuments

- Inspect the Mnemonic Nexus, Axiom monument, Horizon Stabilizer, and other oversized one-block monuments from all sides.
- Confirm the intended multi-block-scale visual silhouette is present even though each monument remains one placed block.
- Confirm removed central pillars/posts remain absent where designed.
- Walk into and around each monument to verify the multipart collision follows its visible form as closely as practical.

## Veilforged equipment

- Equip Phaseweave, Causal, and Sovereign pieces individually and as full sets.
- Confirm each slot renders only its own geometry and material layer.
- Confirm worn geometry animation is smooth and independent of inventory-icon animation.
- Confirm Phaseweave, Causal, and Sovereign material animation remain visually distinct.
- Verify Phaseweave fall-to-DE conversion, Causal lethal rollback/cooldown, and Sovereign Domain abilities.

## Breaches and Veil Lance

- Open breaches with the Veil Lance against each boundary orientation and verify beam/facing alignment.
- Confirm normal and overdrive visual states are distinguishable.
- Confirm breach mist remains neutral/ash-toned and respects camera clearance.
- Take real health damage from each linked Breach creature: Void Crawler should snare movement/mining, Rift Stalker should inflict darkness/weakness, and Null Wraith should drain Core DE, add bounded fracture pressure, and weaken.
- Close/sever a Breach and verify former creatures cannot continue applying linked-Breach combat effects.

## Memories, Laws, and Domain controls

- Confirm a fresh player begins with no learned/captured/archived Memories.
- Exercise Mnemonic Vessel capture, Memory Archive storage, and Domain Orrery application through normal progression.
- Verify Open Sky illumination applies only to the owning Domain and does not leak across other players' Domains.
- Verify Law monument interactions and Domain controls remain owner-authoritative.

## Matter and Veil Inventory

- Confirm items with Matter value deposit as Matter rather than physical stacks.
- Confirm items without Matter value remain as physical Veil Inventory items.
- Verify learned-pattern synthesis and Craftables calculations remain conservative and atomic.
- Exercise Veil Interface automation with owner online, including insertion and extraction.

## Multiplayer isolation

- Test two owners with separate Domains simultaneously.
- Verify boundary state, fog settings, Open Sky illumination, Memories/Laws, Matter, breaches, and Core energy remain isolated per owner.
- Enter another player's Domain as a guest and press the Domain key; verify return to the exact saved guest-entry location.

Record any graphical issue with a screenshot plus the feature, Domain size, viewing distance, and approximate FPS/frame-time change so it can be reproduced against the exact validated build.
